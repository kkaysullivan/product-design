# Action Buttons

Covers get-started, sign-in, and sign-up screens where the action sits
directly below stacked form fields.

**Industry pattern:** most intake forms place the action nav below the
main content area, primary on the right, tertiary on the left — usually a
solid primary button and a tertiary with a left arrow icon.

## SPA

### Desktop

- The action bar is stuck to the bottom of the browser window, but its
  width matches the content area of the page — not the full browser
  window. See
  [Decisions/Navigation and Progress](?path=/docs/decisions-navigation-and-progress--docs)
  for the stable-action-bar decision.
- Primary is always on the right, flush with the right edge of the content
  area.
- Tertiary is always on the left, flush with the left edge of the content
  area, styled as a **subtle button** — no border, no box.
- Intake pages specifically: the action bar's width is set by the main
  content area of the form.
- Do not flush the actions right or left of the entire browser window —
  they become harder to locate. We adhere to
  <a href="https://lawsofux.com/fittss-law/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Fitt's Law</a>.

## Modular

as a **subtle button** — no border, no box.
