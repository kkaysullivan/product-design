# Background & Containers

- Whenever a background box is used to hold content, never call it a
  "card."
- The content area is not the same as the container that houses it.

## SPA

- Intake experiences do not use background boxes. Form content floats
  directly on the page.
- Stay on one treatment for the whole intake flow — don't move between
  boxed and unboxed as the user progresses.
- Because content floats with no box to set it apart, background contrast
  is what has to carry legibility. This is why light backgrounds only
  (below) isn't optional.
- Do not use dark backgrounds.
- Nothing to mix here — there's no boxed option on SPA, so this doesn't
  apply.

<Canvas of={BackgroundAndContainersStories.SpaFloating} />

## Modular

- Must use background boxes to hold content. Intake keeps content in boxes
  only — no floating on the background. Boxes let brand peek through for
  cohesion.
- The container's job is separation: a Modular experience lives inside
  whatever page it's embedded in, so the box is what visually distinguishes
  the form from the host page's surrounding content.
- Splash and results pages may float content on the background.
- Don't mix boxed and bare content within the same screen — each screen
  commits to one or the other.

**Not yet decided:** the container may eventually need both light and dark
treatments, depending on the host page it's embedded in. Today there's
only the one (light) container — don't build a dark variant until this is
locked.

## Modular → SPA (hybrid)

- Modular intake stays boxed right up to the handoff. Once the SPA opens
  for results, it follows the SPA rule above — content floats, no boxes.
  Don't carry the Modular box across the handoff.

## Dark or saturated backgrounds

Not the default intake treatment. The broader direction stays toward
simpler, lighter intake experiences — stronger visual treatments are
reserved for moments with a specific purpose, not applied throughout the
flow.

- Reserved for key emotional moments only. Overuse makes them meaningless,
  and when used, live text must stay accessible.
- Same direction as low-brand intake overall — see
  [Decisions/Page Types](?path=/docs/decisions-page-types--docs) (Brand)
  and the low-bandwidth end of
  [Decisions/Brand Intensity](?path=/docs/decisions-brand-intensity--docs).

## Brand treatment

Subtle brush strokes are fine. Nothing loud, nothing that competes with
the task.
