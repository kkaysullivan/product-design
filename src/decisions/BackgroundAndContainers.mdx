# Background & Containers

- White background boxes hold content.
- Never call them "cards."
- Don't mix boxed content with bare content in the same experience.
- The content area is not the same as the container that houses it.

## SPA

- Do not use background boxes to hold content. Content floats, on light
  backgrounds only.
- Do not use dark backgrounds.

<Canvas of={BackgroundAndContainersStories.SpaFloating} />

## Modular

- Must use background boxes to hold content. Intake keeps content in boxes
  only — no floating on the background. Boxes let brand peek through for
  cohesion.
- Splash and results pages may float content on the background.

<Canvas of={BackgroundAndContainersStories.ModularBoxed} />

## Dark or saturated backgrounds

Reserved for key emotional moments only. Overuse makes them meaningless,
and when used, live text must stay accessible.

<Canvas of={BackgroundAndContainersStories.EmotionalMoment} />

## Brand treatment

Subtle brush strokes are fine. Nothing loud, nothing that competes with
the task.
