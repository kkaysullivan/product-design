# Motion

Transitions stay subtle throughout the intake flow.

## Entry transition

- **SPA only** — big transition once, at entry. Dark-to-light coming from
  RS.com into the experience. Everything inside stays subtle.
- **Modular only** — no big transitions. Keep motion at a minimum, except
  for special screens.

## Easing

One easing curve for everything. The Ramsey ease curve already running in
the portal is the standard.

## Continuity

Animated elements carry context page to page. Logos and content blocks move
with you so it reads as continuity, not a flip.
