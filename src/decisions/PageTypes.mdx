# Page Types

Every page type in the funnel has its own purpose, and its own decisions.
**Everything below applies to Intake pages only.** As Intro, Dashboard,
Results, and Splash pages get their own decisions, they'll get their own
sections on this page.

## Intake

Form intake is for collecting data points, asking for feedback, and
contains interaction. This is the most constrained page.

### Hierarchy

Hierarchy holds — the page scans in under 5 seconds. No change from what's
already established.

### Customer love

- Intake is the functional screen (low delight).
- Emotional design is dialed down on purpose.
- Deep delight could be added by hiding fields we already have the user's
  info for.
