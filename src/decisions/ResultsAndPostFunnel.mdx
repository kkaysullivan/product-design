# Results & Post Funnel

This is the Modular → SPA hybrid decision — see
[Foundations/Experience Types](?path=/docs/foundations-experience-types--docs).
Results never live in Modular; a Modular intake always hands off into a
SPA at this point.

- Results and post funnel pages must load in a SPA experience. No
  exceptions, regardless of whether the intake before it was SPA or
  Modular.

<Canvas of={ResultsAndPostFunnelStories.SpaOnly} />

- Results and dashboards are a completely separate rule set. No action bar.
  Different layout logic.

<Canvas of={ResultsAndPostFunnelStories.SeparateRuleSet} />

- All pro connection and service results land inside Ramsey Connect, never
  a standalone page on RamseySolutions.com.

<Canvas of={ResultsAndPostFunnelStories.ProConnectRouting} />

- The loader while results generate is the transition into results. Don't
  stack a second transition on top of it.

<Canvas of={ResultsAndPostFunnelStories.LoaderTransition} />
