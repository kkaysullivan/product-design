# Spacing & Padding

One main-body padding value, applied across the whole experience, in
increments of 8px. The same value is used for padding within the content
area.

## SPA

- **Mobile** — 3 options only: 8px, 16px, or 24px. 16px and 24px are the
  most common.
- **Desktop** — 13 options only, in 8px steps: 32, 40, 48, 56, 64, 72, 80,
  88, 96, 104, 112, 120, or 128px. 32px and 48px are the most common.

## Modular

- **Mobile and desktop** — 3 options only: 8px, 16px, or 24px, same at
  every breakpoint.
