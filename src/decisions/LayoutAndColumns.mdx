# Layout & Columns

All columns live above a stable action bar.

## Intake form pages

Always 1-column. No exceptions — SPA or Modular, mobile or desktop. This
is the defining intake rule: because the page is gathering input, it stays
single-column. Most important info sits at top, less important info stacks
below.

<Canvas of={LayoutAndColumnsStories.IntakeOneColumn} />

## Splash and intro pages

- **SPA desktop** — can flex between 1-column and 2-column. Left column
  should always be a form. Right content should be contextual.
- All other cases — 1 column.

<Canvas of={LayoutAndColumnsStories.SplashTwoColumn} />

## Modular desktop

Not applicable. Modular always defaults to mobile sizing and treatment —
there is no distinct desktop layout.
