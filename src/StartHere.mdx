# Start Here

[Shared UX working session (Figma) →](https://www.figma.com/board/Ul3L1KiOlylRlHHVP1Aful/Shared-UX?t=hPG3bkJeWQqQiiPL-6)

## What this is

The start of an ongoing conversation about consistency across our experiences.

## Goal

One clear direction for all three screen types, consistent with LTF, patterns, structure, and principles.

## The problem

Term and P&C experiences are inconsistent across products. Intake to application feels disconnected.

## Outcome

A system that scales across every business we support. That means defining a repeatable experience system and thinking through:

- **Skeleton** (layout)
- **Structure** (flow)
- **Surface direction** (guardrails, not final UI)

## Before the meeting

Look at Mobbin for how other companies handle these three page types, then generate several ideas for each:

1. **Form intake.** How errors and education get handled
2. **Pause page.** Matched pro, results screens, etc.
3. **Results page.** Signed-in state

Converge to one strong idea per type. Come ready to discuss:

**Core patterns**
- Layout approach
- CTA behavior
- Step progression model

**Key states**
- Input
- Education
- Decision
- Confirmation

**Rationale**
- Why this works
- What problem it solves
- Where it might break

## Guardrails

Every idea has to work across:

- Multiple businesses (P&C, Term, etc.)
- Mobile and desktop, mobile first
